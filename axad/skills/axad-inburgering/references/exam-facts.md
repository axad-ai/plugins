# The inburgering exam: facts and sources

Checked on 20 November 2026 against the pages linked. "Secondary" marks a claim read on a competitor, an aggregator or a news summary, not on an official page: say it as "reported", never as official. Whatever is not here, do not state as fact.

## Who must take it

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| The **Wet inburgering 2021 (Wi2021)** applies to anyone whose duty began on or after 1 January 2022. The duty starts when the IND publishes the residence right; the 3-year term starts the day after the PIP date. | [government.nl](https://www.government.nl/themes/migration-and-travel/integration-in-the-netherlands/civic-integration-in-the-netherlands), [Divosa](https://www.divosa.nl/publicaties/wet-inburgering-vraag-en-antwoord/algemeen/doelgroep) |
| **Wi2013** still applies to duties that began before 2022. | same |
| **Exempt:** EU, EEA and Swiss citizens; under 18; people past retirement age; temporary residents (work, study); holders of Dutch-taught diplomas. Partners from non-EU countries (family migrants) are obliged. | same |

## Levels by route

| Route | Exams | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Wi2021 **B1 route** | B1 language exams, KNM, MAP, PVT. Scaling down to A2 is possible after at least 600 hours of lessons at a school with the Blik op Werk quality mark. | [DUO Wi2021](https://duo.nl/zakelijk/inburgering-ketenpartners/wet-inburgering-2021/examens-wi2021/examen-doen-wi2021.jsp) |
| Wi2021 **education route** | B1 or B2, KNM, PVT | same |
| Wi2021 **Z-route** | A1, no central exams | same |
| **Wi2013** | A2 language exams, KNM, ONA (duties from October 2017 on), PVT | [inburgeren.nl](https://www.inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/index.jsp) |
| **Naturalisation** (also open to anyone who takes it of their own accord) | an A2 diploma with all parts; a Staatsexamen NT2 diploma (B1 or B2) also counts. The IND lists KNM plus ONA (Wi2013) or MAP (Wi2021) plus PVT; taking it in the Netherlands adds ONA. | [IND](https://ind.nl/en/dutch-citizenship/civic-integration-for-naturalisation) |

**A B1 requirement for naturalisation is planned, not in force.** According to a summary of a Kamervragen answer of 3 November 2026 on [inburgering-examen.nl](https://inburgering-examen.nl/naturalisatie/b1-taaleis) (secondary), consultation is planned for the end of 2026 and there is no date.

## Parts and minutes

[inburgeren.nl, taalexamens](https://www.inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/inhoud-taalexamens-a2-b1-b2.jsp) and [kennisexamens](https://www.inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/inhoud-kennisexamens.jsp):

| Part | A2 (DUO) | B1 / B2 (Staatsexamen NT2) |
|---|---|---|
| Lezen | 65 min, computer | B1 110 min, B2 100 min |
| Luisteren | 45 min, computer | 90 min |
| Spreken | 35 min, computer | about 30 min |
| Schrijven | 40 min, pen and paper, 4 tasks | 100 min |
| KNM | 45 min, computer | the same exam; there is no B1 version |

- **MAP** is done at the municipality, free of charge. **ONA** is a portfolio plus a 64-hour course or a final interview. **PVT** is the participation statement process run by the municipality.
- **KNM changed on 1 July 2025**: new goals (digital communication, the Holocaust, women's self-determination) and a question style that tests knowledge instead of behaviour ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/06/30/inburgeringsexamen-vernieuwd-extra-aandacht-voor-zelfbeschikking-vrouwen-en-kennis-holocaust), [Cito](https://cito.nl/actueel/knm-examen-in-een-nieuw-jasje-beter-moderner-relevanter)). Many old KNM question banks are out of date.
- **Who runs it:** DUO runs registration and the A2 and KNM exams in Mijn Inburgering (DigiD). Cito builds the KNM. The CvTE sets the NT2 exams, which DUO administers at 6 locations.
- **Results:** A2 Spreken and Schrijven take about 16 weeks; the other parts about 8 weeks.
- **Pass marks:** not found on an official page. A KNM pass mark of 28 of 40 and a Listening pass mark of 19 of 25 appear only on competitor sites (secondary): do not state them as official.

## Cost and loans

[inburgeren.nl, betalen](https://www.inburgeren.nl/inburgeren-betalen/index.jsp):

- Wi2021: €50 per part, so €250 for 5 parts. Wi2013 adds ONA at €40, so €290. NT2: €50 per part ([CvTE flyer](https://www.staatsexamensnt2.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2025/06/04/flyer-staatsexamen-nt2/83731_CVTE_FlyerA5-Staatsexamen-NL-ENG.pdf)).
- Asylum status holders: the first 2 attempts per exam are free, and they cannot borrow.
- Everyone else can borrow from DUO for approved schools and exams. Interest is 2.29% in 2026 and 3.28% in 2027.

## Official practice exams

- **A2:** 15 free sets on [inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/oefenen.jsp](https://www.inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/oefenen.jsp), on oefenexamensduo.optimumassessment.com: 3 Schrijven (PDF), 3 Spreken, 3 Luisteren, 4 Lezen, 2 KNM. The KNM sets match the new exam.
- **B1 / B2:** [oefenexamensnt2.nl](https://oefenexamensnt2.nl/): full exams, loose Spreken and Schrijven tasks, downloads.
- DUO's assessment models (beoordelingsmodel) for Spreken and Schrijven A2 are PDFs on the [DUO Wi2021 page](https://duo.nl/zakelijk/inburgering-ketenpartners/wet-inburgering-2021/examens-wi2021/examen-doen-wi2021.jsp): link them, do not paste them.

## Pass rates

Only for context, never as a promise. The CvTE's annual reports give pass rates per sitting, not per person, via the [KlaarNL table](https://klaarnl.com/en/levels-and-exams/staatsexamen-nt2-pass-rates/) (secondary, it cites the PDFs): Staatsexamen NT2 B1 in 2025, per skill: speaking 53%, listening 43%, writing 62%, reading 44%; the B1 average was 51% on 73,374 sittings. There is no first-attempt or per-person rate anywhere, and none for the A2 exams that was checked.

## What Axad may and may not do with official material

- **Safe:** facts (formats, minutes, fees, goals), links to rubrics and official practice, original questions written to the exam goals, and Axad's own scoring.
- **Not safe:** copying, re-hosting or embedding the DUO and NT2 practice exams (the Ministry reserves copyright; the "educational purposes" exception is unclear for a paid tool), and any "leaked" real exam question: A2 candidates promise not to share the exam ([Regels voor het examen](https://www.inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/regels-voor-het-examen.jsp)).
